**--> BUSINESS INSIGHTS**



Based on the analysis performed using Python, SQL, and Power BI, the following key business insights were identified:



1\. Delivery Performance Impacts Customer Satisfaction

 Late delivery has a strong negative impact on customer satisfaction. Orders delivered early received an average review score of 4.29, while late-delivered orders received an average review score of 2.57.



2\. Majority of Orders Were Delivered Early

  Out of the analyzed orders, 88,658 orders were delivered early, whereas 7,701 orders were delivered late. This indicates that although most deliveries performed well, late deliveries still represent an important area for improvement.



3\. Late Deliveries Require Special Attention

The significant difference between early and late delivery review scores indicates that reducing delivery delays can potentially improve customer satisfaction and overall customer experience.



4\. Seller Location Influences Delivery Performance

Delivery performance varies across seller states. Some seller locations show comparatively higher late-delivery rates, indicating that geographical location and logistics efficiency can influence delivery outcomes.



5\. Payment Methods Show Different Customer Spending Patterns

Average Order Value varies across different payment methods. This indicates that customer purchasing behavior differs depending on the selected payment method.



6\. Customer Reviews Can Be Used as a Performance Indicator

 Review scores provide useful feedback about the overall customer experience. Lower review scores associated with late deliveries indicate that customer reviews can help identify delivery-related problems.



7\. Delivery Experience Is an Important Business Factor

  The analysis shows that logistics and delivery performance are not only operational metrics but also directly connected with customer satisfaction. Improving delivery reliability can therefore contribute to better customer experience and business performance.
