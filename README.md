##E-Commerce Sales \& Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Project Connector is an end-to-end E-Commerce Sales and Customer Analytics project developed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, order delivery performance, payment patterns, seller performance, and customer satisfaction.

The project uses data analysis and business intelligence techniques to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights through Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall e-commerce sales and order performance.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Analyze delivery performance and late deliveries.
* Identify the relationship between delivery performance and customer satisfaction.
* Analyze seller and geographical performance.
* Compare customer spending patterns across payment methods.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
* Generate actionable business recommendations.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### 1\. Delivery Performance \& Customer Satisfaction

Early-delivered orders received an average review score of **4.29**, while late-delivered orders received an average review score of **2.57**.

This indicates a strong relationship between delivery experience and customer satisfaction.

### 2\. Delivery Performance

* Early Delivered Orders: **88,658**
* Late Delivered Orders: **7,701**

Although the majority of analyzed orders were delivered early, late deliveries remain an important area for operational improvement.

### 3\. Seller Location Performance

Delivery performance varies across seller states. Some locations have comparatively higher late-delivery rates, indicating potential geographical and logistics-related challenges.

### 4\. Payment Behavior

Average Order Value varies across payment methods, showing differences in customer purchasing behavior.

### 5\. Customer Reviews

Customer review scores provide useful feedback about the overall shopping and delivery experience and can be used to identify service-quality issues.
